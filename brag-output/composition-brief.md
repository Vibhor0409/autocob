# Hyperframes Composition Brief: AutoCOB

- Composition: `brag-output/composition/`, render to `brag-output/brag.mp4`, 1920x1080, 60s.
- Source: landing page (`app/page.tsx`), design tokens (`app/globals.css`), screens captured by `scripts/capture-shots.mjs`.
- Visual identity: mint-to-sky wash (#eef5f5 with teal and sky radial glows), frosted white panels, ink #10222c, brand teal #0c8a7b, claim-form red #c4122f for stuck money only. Schibsted Grotesk for text, Spline Sans Mono for figures (the app's own fonts, shipped locally).
- Storyboard: see `brag-plan.md`.
- Audio: one warm music bed with fade in and out, no SFX, no voice.
- Requirements: real product screens in scene 5; every number sourced or from the repo; all text holds long enough to read.
